"Samatvam Yoga Ucyate" — Bhagavad Gita
The Spirit of Samatvam

Equanimity of Mind, Strength of Body, Clarity of Soul

In Sanskrit, Samatvam means a steady, balanced mind, whatever is happening around us. I am simply a medium for sharing what I have learned from my teachers and my own practice. These teachings belong to the ancient tradition of yoga, and I share them with gratitude, hoping they bring you a little more calm and ease.

Breath

Simple breathing practices (pranayama) to calm the mind and bring a feeling of lightness and energy.

Mindful Movement

Gentle postures (asanas) practiced with awareness, at your own pace and within your own comfort.
